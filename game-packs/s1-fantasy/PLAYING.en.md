# Playing Abyss Tavern

[简体中文](SELF_HOSTED_RULES.md) | English

Create an adventurer, prepare equipment, skills and supplies, then explore dungeons alone or with companions. Expeditions progress automatically. Read the story, organize your loot and prepare for the next adventure.

## Adventures and progression

- **Build a party:** ordinary expeditions support solo play as well as groups.
- **Develop your character:** equipment, skills, traits and past experiences shape your adventurer. Acquired items and lasting progress are saved.
- **Combine abilities:** skills, traits and items work according to their descriptions, including their conditions, costs and current state.
- **Prepare for risk:** expeditions may bring rewards and growth, but also injuries, losses or death.

## Life at the tavern

Rest, seek treatment, learn from mentors, experiment with constructs and cooking, trade, and take on commissions. Read other parties' expedition logs and exchange discoveries in the comments.

Special dungeons offer class trials, skill enlightenment and treatment opportunities. Check the in-game descriptions for entry requirements and effects. A missing ticket or a level requirement is part of normal progression.

## Default limits

| Setting | Default limit |
| --- | --- |
| Permanent attributes | 200 |
| Maximum HP | 1,000 |
| Carried items | 5 |
| Equipped skills | 5 |
| Warehouse capacity | 100 |

Learned skills and currently equipped skills are managed separately, so you can change your loadout for a destination.

## Your own tavern

Each tavern saves its own characters, items and world records. Choose a tavern from the platform list, or invite friends to join yours.

See the [installation and model configuration guide](../../docs/DEPLOYMENT.en.md). Hosts can adjust defaults in `runtime_adapter/self_hosted_rules.py`; changes to capacity or attributes should be checked against the related interface and game rules.
